Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

PageStack (formerly Merge & Split PDF Pro) is a PDF toolkit for iPhone and iPad. Every tool runs on your device. Your documents, photos, signatures and passwords are never uploaded, and there is no account. The app sends two kinds of anonymous data, described below: crash reports and usage counts.

Your files

  • Files you import or make are kept in PageStack's own folder on your device, which you can see in the Files app (On My iPhone › PageStack). If you turn on the iCloud Drive library in Settings, that folder is kept in your own iCloud Drive by Apple; we never receive it.
  • Merging, splitting, compressing, protecting, signing, filling forms, marking up, text recognition and Office to PDF all happen on your device. Office documents and web pages are converted with network access blocked, so links and pictures inside them are not loaded from the internet.
  • File names and page counts are added to your device's Spotlight index so you can search for them; this index stays on your device.
  • Deleted files stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you scan a document or take a photo for a PDF.
  • Photos: you choose photos in Apple's picker, so PageStack can't see your other photos. Saving pages as images asks for permission to add to your library only.

Signatures and passwords

Signatures you draw or take from a photo are stored on your device only. Passwords you type to protect or unlock a PDF are used for that file and never stored or sent.

Crash reports

If the App Store or TestFlight version of PageStack crashes or stops responding, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains technical details: the app version, the code that was running, the device model, iOS version, memory and similar device state, and a random install identifier. About one in ten sessions also sends performance timings.

A report does not contain your name, email, documents, file names, photos, signatures, screenshots or a recording of the screen. IP addresses are not stored. Reports are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Anonymous usage counts

PageStack counts how the app is used with TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH), so we know which tools to improve: which screens and tools are opened, which options are chosen, whether a task finished or failed (with a fixed reason), and rough counts such as "6 to 20 pages". Counts use a random identifier that is hashed on your device and are not linked to you.

They never include file names, folder names, document contents, text you type, search words, passwords, signatures or file sizes. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses, and the counts are not used for advertising or tracking.

Send Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, your message, and your email address only if you enter one for a reply, are sent to the developer through Sentry's feedback service, together with the app version and device model. It is used only to read and answer your message.

What PageStack does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.

Children

PageStack is a general tool, not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
