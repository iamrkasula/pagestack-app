Support

Need help? The answers below cover the common questions, and the contact options are at the bottom.

Common questions

Where are my files?

In the Library tab, and in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › PageStack. Turn on the iCloud Drive library in Settings to keep them in iCloud Drive instead.

I deleted a file by mistake.

Open Library › Recently Deleted and tap Restore. Deleted files are kept for 30 days.

How do I merge PDFs and photos?

Tap Merge PDF on Home, add files from the Library, Files, Photos or a scan, drag them into order, then tap Merge.

How do I take out some pages?

Use Split PDF and type a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-, or tap the pages you want. Organize can also pull selected pages into a new file.

A PDF asks for a password.

PageStack asks for the password when it needs to read the file. If you don't know it, the file can't be opened; there's no way around a PDF password.

How do I sign a document?

Open Sign, choose the PDF, tap Signature to draw one (or take it from a photo of your signature on paper), then tap the page where it goes. Add the date or initials the same way.

Can I open files from Mail or Safari?

Yes. Use Share › PageStack (or Open in) on any PDF or image, then pick a tool.

Does PageStack upload my documents?

No. Every tool works on your device. See the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Please tell us your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, what you did and what happened instead. A screenshot or screen recording helps (leave out anything private).
